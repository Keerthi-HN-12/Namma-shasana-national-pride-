# **Namma-Shasane 🏛️**

## AI-Driven Heritage Conservation & Stone Inscription Interpretation Platform

An advanced Android application built to recognize, interpret, preserve, and digitally map Karnataka’s ancient stone inscriptions (“Shasanas”) using Artificial Intelligence and location-based heritage mapping.

---

# 📌 **Project Summary**

Namma-Shasane connects historical heritage with modern digital technology by converting ancient inscriptions into searchable and understandable digital records.

This system enables users to locate nearby inscriptions, capture stone carvings through images, translate them using AI models, and contribute to a community-driven heritage preservation network.

The goal is to safeguard Karnataka’s rich cultural and historical identity while improving accessibility, education, and heritage tourism.

---

# ✨ **Core Features**

## 📷 Inscription Capture & Recognition

* Take photos or upload images of ancient stone inscriptions for processing.

## 🌐 AI-Powered Text Interpretation

* Convert historical scripts into readable Kannada or English translations.

## 🗺️ Smart Heritage Mapping

* Discover and explore nearby inscription sites using GPS-based tracking.

## 🏺 Historical Context Explorer

* View dynasty information, cultural background, and historical significance.

## 🚨 Heritage Protection Reporting

* Report damaged, neglected, or at-risk heritage monuments.

## 🔍 Advanced Search System

* Search inscriptions using keywords, locations, or historical categories.

# 📱 **System Workflow**

1. User captures an image of a stone inscription
2. OCR engine extracts textual data from the image
3. AI translation module processes and converts the text
4. Historical insights are generated and displayed
5. Geographic location is stored on an interactive map
6. Users can submit preservation alerts or updates
